# Anti-generic AI audit

Use this audit when a landing page needs to feel intentional, authored, and
specific to its product rather than assembled from familiar generated parts.

## Signals to investigate

These signals matter most when several appear together without a clear product
or brand reason:

- an oversized centred headline, short vague subhead, and one pill CTA;
- indigo/violet gradient, blurred mesh/noise background, and frosted cards;
- identical rounded cards arranged as an automatic three-column feature row;
- default typography used at predictable weights and scales everywhere;
- decorative badges such as “AI 99%”, “live”, “new”, “showcase”, or fake
  activity/status notifications;
- a standard logo-left, three-links-centre, CTA-right header regardless of the
  page type;
- generic feature names, synthetic proof, emoji icons, or dashboard fragments
  that do not demonstrate a real outcome;
- motion applied everywhere, especially drifting blobs, endless marquee rows,
  and entrance effects with no interaction purpose.

Do not treat any single item as forbidden. A verified status can be useful; a
glass surface can suit a selected system. The failure is unexamined accumulation
and repetition.

## Corrective pass

1. Restate the visitor’s task and the page’s single conversion goal.
2. Remove every decorative element that does not improve comprehension, trust,
   wayfinding, or the chosen brand expression.
3. Replace generic content groups with the user-selected section sequence and
   real evidence. Merge or remove duplicate cards.
4. Give typography a product-specific role: choose a readable body face, a
   purposeful display treatment, and a restrained scale rather than relying on
   a familiar default.
5. Give surfaces hierarchy: use flat, bordered, textured, editorial, or
   dimensional treatments only where they separate meaningful layers.
6. Make navigation match the information architecture; a landing page may need
   a compact utility bar, an editorial masthead, an in-page index, or no
   persistent navigation.
7. Keep motion tied to feedback, orientation, or emphasis. Test reduced motion
   and remove motion that only signals “modern”.

## Pass condition

The page should still communicate its offer, proof, and next action when all
decorative effects are turned off. A reviewer should be able to name why its
layout, type, palette, sections, and navigation fit this specific product.
