# Landing strategy

Inspect the product and research its category before proposing structure. For a
redesign, audit the existing experience and preserve real brand constraints.

Create a compact strategy artifact with:

- primary audience and its job-to-be-done;
- one concrete offer and one primary CTA;
- a proof inventory containing only supplied, verifiable facts;
- message hierarchy from promise to action;
- objections and the appropriate evidence or explanation;
- a responsive section sequence; and
- assumptions and unanswered questions.

Do not invent testimonials, customer logos, customer counts, awards, security
claims, or performance claims. If proof is weak, recommend honest substitutes:
product demonstration, process transparency, founder expertise already supplied,
or a clear invitation to talk.

Use hierarchy, proximity, progressive disclosure, and clear labels. Aesthetic
novelty cannot compensate for a hidden offer or ambiguous CTA.
