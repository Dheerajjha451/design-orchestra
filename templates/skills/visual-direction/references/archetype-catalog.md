# Archetype selection notes

The package exports structured archetype data for: functional, editorial and
luxury minimalism; maximalist collage; Swiss; Bauhaus; neo-brutalist; magazine
editorial; Art Deco; retro Y2K; retro-futurist; organic; playful; cinematic;
typographic poster; industrial; warm humanist; monochrome; dark tech;
cyberpunk; translucent glass; soft dimensional; bento; and kinetic
experimental.

Each archetype has suitability, compatibility, anti-patterns, accessibility
risk, and seven numerical axes. Use it to reject inappropriate combinations,
not to mechanically produce an aesthetic. High-risk directions need an
explicit legibility, contrast, focus, and reduced-motion plan.
